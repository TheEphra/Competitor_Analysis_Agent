05 — HOW TO READ THIS

Useful signals, honest limits.

A quick explanation of what the analysis can—and can’t—tell you.

01

Measured

Page title, meta description, headings, visible text, contact signals, and HTTPS.

02

Score coverage

The score reflects available checks. Missing signals are excluded, and coverage shows what could be measured.

03

Not measured

Traffic, rankings, reviews, conversion rates, advertising, and market share are outside this version.

Only submit public websites you’re permitted to access. Google Places searches may incur charges. Website scores are heuristic indicators, not business rankings.
